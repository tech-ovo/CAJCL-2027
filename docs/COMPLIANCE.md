## 1. Executive Summary: How the ID-Only Model Transforms Compliance

By replacing student first and last names with a **CAJCL-assigned Delegate ID** (alongside the platform's existing ban on student email addresses, home addresses, dates of birth, SSNs, payment card data, and digital health records), the web database holds **zero direct personal identifiers for students**.

Under federal and California privacy statutes, this architectural choice shifts student records from **Personally Identifiable Information (PII)** to **de-identified / pseudonymous operational records**:
1. **Eliminates COPPA Registration Consent Friction:** An assigned ID, grade (6–12), Latin level, and hashed login token used solely to run convention scheduling and testing do not constitute "personal information" under 16 CFR § 312.2 (or fall squarely within COPPA's "support for internal operations" exception for persistent identifiers under 16 CFR § 312.5(c)(7)). Middle school students (ages 11–12) can self-register via `#/join` without triggering prior parental consent requirements for registration data.
2. **Satisfies FERPA's De-Identified Record Standard:** Under 34 CFR § 99.31(b)(1), teachers (sponsors) may share de-identified student records coded with an assigned ID without violating FERPA, provided the sponsor retains the `Student Name ↔ Assigned ID` mapping key at the school level.
3. **Neutralizes Statutory Data Breach Triggers for Student DB Records:** California's breach notification statute (Cal. Civ. Code § 1798.82) defines covered "personal information" around an individual's **first name or first initial and last name** combined with sensitive elements or account credentials. Without student names in the database, a database incident cannot expose student names paired with any statutory element.

## 3. Policy Positions Under the ID-Only Model

### 3.1 Middle & High School Self-Registration via Chapter Join Codes (`#/join`)
- **How It Works Without Student Names:**
  1. Each chapter receives an 8-character plaintext join code (`XXXX-XXXX`) printed on the sponsor's handout.
  2. When a student visits `#/join` and enters their chapter's join code, they select only their **Academic Grade (6–12)** and **Latin Level** (no first name, no last name, no email, no phone number).
  3. The platform assigns the student a unique **Delegate ID / Badge Number** (e.g., `Delegate #1042` or school-scoped seat number) and displays their one-time **Login Token** (`DEL-XXXXX-XXXXX`).
  4. The student writes their **Assigned Delegate ID** and **Login Token** on their physical paper waiver/registration sheet and hands the signed paper waiver to their sponsor (or the sponsor records the student's name next to that Delegate ID on the teacher's private local key).
  5. In the portal's *Waiting for approval* panel, the sponsor verifies that the Assigned ID matches a real student on their paper sheet and clicks **Approve** (promoting the ID to the active, billable roster) or **Deny** (which permanently deletes the pending ID and all associated selections).
- **Why This Solves Under-13 COPPA Concerns:** Because the student never transmits a name, email, phone number, or photo during registration, **zero PII is collected online during join-code self-registration**. Even the strictest school district privacy officers permit anonymous/ID-coded test and activity sign-up portals.

### 3.2 At-Convention Photo Contest & Convention Photography (Maximum Permissible Scope)
To allow the broadest possible student participation in the photo contest while staying within the law:
- **Legal Basis for Broad Participation:**
  1. **Cal. Civ. Code § 3344 Is Limited to Commercial Use:** California's statutory right-of-publicity law bars using someone's photograph on merchandise or in commercial advertising without consent, and explicitly exempts non-commercial educational and organizational events. Running an internal 501(c)(3) convention contest and projecting winning photos at the live Awards Assembly is non-commercial.
  2. **FERPA Does Not Restrict Student-Taken Event Photos:** Photos taken by students on their phones at a state convention are not official school "education records" under 20 U.S.C. § 1232g.
  3. **Physical Parent Waiver Covers Identifiable Faces Under COPPA:** Even though the portal stores no student names, a photo showing a child's face is treated as PII under COPPA. Including a standard **Convention Photography & Media Release** clause on the mandatory physical **Student Waiver & Permission Form** signed by every parent prior to check-in provides verifiable parental consent for:
     - Appearing in convention photographs taken by peers or staff during official convention events.
     - Displaying winning photo contest entries at the General Assembly / Awards Ceremony or in convention publications.
- **Technical & Operational Guardrails:**
  - **ID-Only Attribution:** Photo uploads in Drive (`Photo Contest/<category>/`) are tagged only with the uploader's **Chapter + Assigned Delegate ID** and optional caption—never a student's name.
  - **Automatic EXIF/GPS Stripping:** Browser re-encoding and `backend/lib/photos.py` strip all location, camera, and timestamp metadata before storage.
  - **Private Storage & One-Click Takedown:** Only the `activities` chair and Convention Presidents can view submitted photos (`#/photo-contest`). Students can withdraw their photo at any time, the chair can take down any photo immediately, and the entire Drive folder is deleted on **April 12, 2027**.

## 4. Codebase & Operational Adjustments Needed for the ID-Only Student Model

Because the existing codebase was originally built with `first_name` and `last_name` on the `people` table, transitioning students to an **ID-Only** model simplifies privacy compliance and requires the following targeted adjustments:

| Component | Current Behavior in Codebase | Adjustment for ID-Only Student Model |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Student Database Records (`people` table)** | Stores `first_name`, `last_name`, `guardian_name`, and `guardian_phone` for delegates (`docs/PRIVACY.md` § 3). | • Leave adult (`SPO` / `VOL`) names and contact info in place so sponsors and chaperones can be reached.<br>• For delegates (`DEL`), omit or auto-populate `first_name` / `last_name` with the assigned identifier (e.g., `Delegate #1042` or school-local sequence `UNI-014`) and keep `guardian_name` / `guardian_phone` strictly on the physical paper medical/waiver form rather than in the web database. |
| **Self-Registration (`#/join`, `js/pages/join.js`, `lib/joining.py`)** | Prompts student for First Name, Last Name, Grade, and Latin Level; blocks duplicate names in the same chapter. | • Prompt student only for **Join Code, Grade (6–12), and Latin Level** (plus an optional sponsor-issued seat/student number if desired to help the teacher match the row).<br>• Display the assigned **Delegate ID** prominently alongside the one-time `DEL-XXXXX-XXXXX` login token so the student can write that ID on their paper waiver form for the sponsor to approve. |
| **Sponsor Roster Import (`#/roster`, `lib/roster.py`)** | Sponsor pastes a list of student names to bulk-create delegates. | • Allow sponsors to either **provision N delegate slots** in one click (generating N Assigned IDs + Login Tokens on a printable handout where the sponsor writes each student's name by hand or via a client-side-only print overlay that never sends names to the API) or paste a count/level list. |
| **Olympika, Potluck & Contest Views** | Displays entrant/helper/submitter `first_name` and `last_name` to the Athletics Director, Activities Chair, and Registration Chair. | • Display **Assigned Delegate ID + Chapter + Grade/Level** (e.g., `Delegate #1042 — University HS`).<br>• Name Drive contest and photo uploads by `{chapter}-{delegate_id}` instead of student names. |
| **Post-Convention Purge (`April 12, 2027`)** | Requires scrubbing student names and emergency contacts from `people` and `audit_log` to create the de-identified archive. | • Student rows in the database are **already de-identified from Day 1**.<br>• The April 12, 2027 purge only needs to delete adult contact info (`SPO`/`VOL` emails and phones), login/session hashes, and the Google Drive folders containing contest submissions, photo contest images, and scanned paper waivers/medical forms. |
